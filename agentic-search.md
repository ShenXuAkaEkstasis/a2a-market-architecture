# Agentic Search Optimization (ASO)

**Shen Xu (徐慎)** · [Home](index.md) · [Unified framework](framework.md)

ASO evaluates whether businesses and commercial offerings can be discovered, assessed and meaningfully considered by AI agents.

## Six dimensions

1. **Discover** — Can an agent find the relevant entity or offer?
2. **Understand** — Can it interpret content, attributes and conditions?
3. **Verify** — Can it substantiate claims with credible evidence?
4. **Qualify** — Does the offer meet eligibility and user constraints?
5. **Select** — Can it make a defensible comparison or recommendation?
6. **Act** — Is an appropriate, authorized next step available?

**Being cited is not the same as being recommended, selected or transacted with.**
