# A2A Commerce and Market Architecture

[Home](index.md) · [Unified framework](framework.md) · [Original research agenda](research-agenda.md)

A2A Commerce studies economic interaction in which AI agents represent or assist commercial participants across discovery, evaluation, coordination, negotiation and transactions. The market concept is **not equivalent to any one A2A communication protocol**.

## Six research areas

1. Market participants — buyer and seller agents, merchants, platforms and intermediaries.
2. Merchant interfaces — structured and usable commercial information.
3. Discovery and distribution — from human traffic to agent-mediated demand.
4. Commercial state — offers, price, inventory, eligibility and availability.
5. Transactions — authorization, identity, payment, fulfillment and remedies.
6. Market economics — value capture, attribution, bargaining power and market power.

## Participation maturity

**Discoverable → Interpretable → Eligible → Transactable → Operable**

Visibility is not selection; technical transaction capability is not proof of permission to transact.

## Technologies to examine

MCP, agent-to-agent communication protocols, Agent Payments Protocol (AP2), Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), and Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) are useful contextual examples. They solve different integration, coordination, authorization or transaction problems. This framework does not claim that they endorse its terminology or model.
