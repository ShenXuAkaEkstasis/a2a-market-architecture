# Unified Research Framework

**Agentic Commerce Market Architecture · Shen Xu (徐慎) · Working v1.0, October 2026**

[Home](index.md) · [DAE](decision-authority.md) · [ASO](agentic-search.md) · [A2A](a2a-commerce.md) · [Publications](publications.md)

Agentic commerce is more than conversational e-commerce. As AI agents discover, assess, select and act on commercial offerings, the key questions concern **decision authority, merchant readiness and market interactions**.

| Research lens | Central question | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Decision Authority Economy (DAE)** | Who has the authority to choose and act, within which constraints? | Conceptual foundation |
| **Agentic Search Optimization (ASO)** | Can agents find, understand, verify, qualify, select and act on an offering? | Applied evaluation |
| **A2A Commerce** | How do commercial participants and agents interact across markets? | Market structure |

These are related research lenses, not a mandated technical stack or universal industry standard.

## Two distinct progressions

**Merchant participation maturity:** Discoverable → Interpretable → Eligible → Transactable → Operable.

**ASO assessment:** Discover → Understand → Verify → Qualify → Select → Act.

The first concerns participation capability; the second concerns how an agent evaluates an offering. Technical access does not, by itself, grant decision authority.

## Boundary conditions

Information access, memory, recommendation, selection and authorized execution are different things. Any real commercial deployment requires attention to identity, mandate, consent, constraints, revocation, auditability and accountability.

## Research status

This is an evolving author-developed framework based on market observation, comparative analysis, technical literature and experiments. It is not presented as an established industry standard. References to protocols or platforms do not imply their endorsement.
